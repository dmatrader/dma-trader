# DMA Trader

Focused tools for active day traders and professional trading teams.

DMA Trader is a trading workspace built for US equities day traders, proprietary trading firms (Prop Firms), T+0 traders, trading teams, and professional trading rooms.

Intraday trading requires clarity, speed, and consistency. DMA Trader is designed to reduce unnecessary friction in the daily workflow, helping traders stay focused on market information, trading decisions, account management, and execution.

We are building a practical, trader-centered product for both independent traders and professional teams. Our focus is a clear experience, dependable workflows, and tools that fit the rhythm of real-time trading without adding unnecessary noise.

We welcome conversations with active traders, Prop Firms, trading teams, and potential product or business partners. If you are interested in product collaboration, business cooperation, early access, or sharing feedback, please open an Issue in this repository, or start a Discussion.

DMA Trader is a software tool for trading workflows. It does not provide investment advice or guarantee trading results.
